Hear It From Them

Questions?

A few things people usually ask before we start.

What's your creative philosophy?

+
I want the work to actually stick with people. So I care more about retention, emotion, and how a story flows than about flashy transitions. Every cut is there for a reason.

Do you sign NDAs?

+
Yes, happily. I work on confidential launches all the time, so I'm glad to sign an NDA before we get into anything.

How many revisions do you offer?

+
Every project comes with 2 rounds of revisions, which is usually plenty to get things exactly right. If you need more, we can always add them.

What's your turnaround time?

+
Short form like Reels and TikToks usually takes 1 to 3 business days. Long form like YouTube takes 3 to 7, depending on how much footage there is and how complex the edit gets.

Do you work alone or with a team?

+
The creative work like cutting, story, and grading is all me. I like keeping full control over the quality. I do lean on junior editors for organising footage and syncing so things move quickly.
